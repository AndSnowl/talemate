Nouns nounsChange…

Nouns are masculine or feminine; you can usually tell by the ending: words ending in -a or -ya are feminine, the rest masculine. Adjectives and the word for "the" follow the noun's gender. There's no separate word for "the": it's an ending added to the noun (see Articles live on the noun).

Verbs verbsChange…

Verbs change for who is acting and when. "To be" is left out in the present ("I cold" means "I am cold") but has past and future forms.
