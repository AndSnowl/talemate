How it sounds Edit

Heavy, rolling words with soft y-glides (ye, yo, ya) and clusters at the front like zv-, str- and khv-. Words often end on -av, -ov or -ar, and the stress sits near the end, so speech sounds weighty and slow, like talk by a stove in a long winter.

How names are built Change…

A person has three names: their own name, their father's name with -ovik (son of) or -ovna (daughter of), and a family name ending in -in, -ov or -evsky.
