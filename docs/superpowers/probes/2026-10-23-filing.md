## How the world was shown

The store still has spheres, drawers and `part_of`. The probe shows it under the new model instead, from the labels file:
- **Every entry is a plain entry,** with one hand-picked tag (the obvious one: river, capital, nation, council, order, custom, technique…) and its filings ("in", with roles). The former spheres read as their kind: the Tsardom a place, the Night Court, the Order, Runic Magic and Velikoye Culture lore. - **The thin stubs are left out of every list** (20 entries: the Night Court's ten role and topic stubs, Chief Scribe, Registered Scribe, The Tsar, The Grand Chiseler, Rune Pattern Approval, and duplicates), as if the world had been made under the new rule.

Prose and the same-thing verdict are off.

**Entry lines** ("Entries near this one"): `Name (kind, tag; usually called X; in A (role), B; stub): summary`, cut at 130 characters. **Tags in use**: every tag on a listed or unlisted live entry except the case's, with its count, most used first: `city (7), nation (4), custom (3), …`.

**The candidate list.** A dry run showed the app's `nearby_ids` gives a stripped stub almost nothing (the Academy's list had 4 entries and lacked Velikoye and Runic Magic), because a stub without `part_of` has no links. The probe uses filing-based nearness instead, as the new app would: the entries whose facts name the stub (most mentions first), then what the prompt names, then their neighbours one step away over filings (either way, weight 2) and fact references (either way, weight 1), most-linked first, at most **24**. Every expected target was in its list except one: **the Frosted Wing in the Frostwing Host case** (it is filed in the Tsardom and names the Training Grounds, not the Host, so one step from the Host's context isn't enough).

**Kinds and temperatures, as the app sets them.** Create, with or without a hint: `creative_instruction_3072` (the creative_instruction preset, 0.85; the client log confirms 0.85). Group synopsis: `analyze` (the analytical preset, 0.7), as prose is written. Group "+ Add": `creative_instruction_1024`, Fill's kind, since "+ Add" replaces Fill. The scene: the narrator's own `narrate_512`.

**Reading the scores.** The bar is at least 80% right and at most 5% false. For filings, the owner's "either side" reading counts a filing between the right pair in the wrong direction (Wattles "in" Wattlemaking, where Wattlemaking belongs in Wattles) as not false; strict counts it false. "Either" targets in the labels may be offered or not.

6. The names rule (replacing today's), then the named-only rule. The parenthesis after the first "tag" is v2's, which fixed the aka-tag collision:

```jinja
- "names": after the facts, every proper name your facts use, other than this {{ kind }}'s own: people, places, peoples and species, named events, named groups and named objects, new or already in the world. Each is {"name", "kind", "tag", "in", "aka"}: "name" in full, as your facts write it; "kind" one of character, place, species, race, event, item, lore (a country, realm or city is a place; a faith, order, council, guild, culture, field of knowledge or magic, law, script or language is lore); "tag" what it is, as for the {{ kind }} (never "proper" or "common": those are only for other names); "in" the name of the entry it belongs in (this {{ kind }}, an entry listed above, or another of your names), or null; "aka" any other names your facts use for the same thing. - Only named things become entries. A role, rank or office (a harbour master, the keeper of the seals), a topic (grain tariffs, winter trade) or a practice without a proper name of its own (the evening bell, night sessions) is never one of the "names": it stays a fact of the {{ kind }}.
```

```jinja
{%- if nearby %}

- "in": the entries listed above that the {{ kind }} belongs in, each {"entry", "role"}: "entry" the entry's name as the list writes it; "role" a few words for how it belongs there ("flows through", "sovereign", "practitioner", "branch of", "taught here"), or null. - "file_into": the entries listed above that belong in this {{ kind }}: its parts, members, branches or what it holds, each {"entry", "role"}, "role" how that entry belongs in this {{ kind }}, or null. Never an entry you put in "in". Only entries from the list; an empty list is fine.
{%- endif %}
```

### Reply shape and code rules

```json
{"name": "...", "aka": [...], "tag": "river", "facts": [...],
 "names": [{"name": "...", "kind": "place", "tag": "...", "in": "<name>" | null, "aka": []}],
 "in": [{"entry": "<name>", "role": "..." | null}],
 "file_into": [{"entry": "<name>", "role": "..." | null}],
 "elsewhere": [...]}
```

- Resolve `entry` names as elsewhere does (name or other name, any case, a leading article dropped). Drop any that don't resolve.
- **Drop a name as a stub** when its `kind` or `tag` is office, title or rank.
- An **existing** name whose `in` is this entry joins "file into". Any other `in` on an existing name is ignored.
- A `kind` outside the list falls back as today.
