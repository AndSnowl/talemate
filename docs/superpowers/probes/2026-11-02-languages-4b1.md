## a. Fill them in, on Claude Code (Opus)

One `claude -p` call per language (no tools). Each got its grammar sections and tables, the `NameGrammar` schema, and the change kinds and conditions. Each reply was scored by whether the engine rebuilds the language's own nouns table (more than one; the; the plural with "the").

| Language | Table rebuilt | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tsevrani, Varsenyk, Skrathik, Moscavian, Varovni | 3/3 | — |
| Bumbagoo | 1/3 by the score, **3/3 really** | The scorer read the table's "a" row as "the". The grammar is right: bumpa → bumpaga, bubumpa. |
| Drassonais | 1/3 by the score, 2/3 really | Its plural row includes "quelc" ("some"), a scorer miss. **A real gap:** "the" is lo for one and lou for more than one, and the engine's conditions can't say "plural". |

**Decision:**
- Fill them in uses this prompt's wording (in Task 7's `names` brief).
- The engine gains a `plural` condition: true when "the" is applied to a word made plural. Drassonais "the": `[{when: plural, word_before "lou"}, {when: before_vowel, word_before "l'"}, {word_before "lo"}]`.

## b. Splitting meanings and Suggest, on Skyfall (koboldcpp, Skyfall-31B-v4.2 Q4_K_M)

**Round 1, the model splits everything: 7/20.** The errors were systematic:
- the noun left plural ("stars", "rivers");
- `joined` set for "white lake" and "grey water";
- "of" and "the" put among the describing words;
- invalid endings ("of", "of the"), and "land of" where there's no "land".

**Round 2, the English read in code, and the model only for one-word compounds: 19/20.**
- In code: "of" and "the" from the words; the last content word is the noun, made singular, and marks "more than one" when it was plural; the words after "of" in "Owl-of-Frost"; "land" read as the "land of" ending.
- A one-word compound ("Blackwater", "Stormwatch") goes to the model, which splits it into noun and describing words.
- The miss, "ashen", is ambiguous (ash with an ending, or a describing word), and acceptable.

**Suggest, round 1: poor.** It returned entity names ("Tsevrani", "Eared Owl-folk") and long describing lists.

**Round 2, asking for ONE common English noun, never a proper name: all seven fit.**
- Drassone: "field" (a pastoral nation).
- Eared Owl-folk: "threshold" (a liminal sense of belonging).
- Snowy Owl-folk, Tsevranyan Owl, Tsardom of Tsevranya: "snow".
- Drassonais: "tongue".
- Tsevrani: "forest".

## Decisions for Tasks 6 and 7

- **The client:** the world model (Skyfall), the spec's default. No fallback is needed for splitting or Suggest.
- **`split_meaning`:** the deterministic English reading above, which the owner's words control. The model is asked only for one-word compounds, in the same call that picks coined words when there are any.
- **`suggest_phrases`:** the round-2 prompt. One common noun per meaning part, with the part's hint, normalised to lower-case singular. The slot's forms come from the part, never from the model.
- **The engine:** add the `plural` condition (Drassonais).
- **Fill them in:** the round-1 prompt's wording, with the conditions and change kinds listed, plus `plural`.
